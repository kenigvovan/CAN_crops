# Ideas for CAN Crops mechanics

## Genetics

### Heterosis (hybrid vigor)
When Dominant and Recessive alleles differ significantly, give a bonus to harvest/growth.
Currently Recessive alleles are barely used - this would make them meaningful.

### Epistasis (gene interaction)
Genes interact with each other:
- High Growth + Low Resistance = disease chance
- High Gain + High Strength = bonus rare drop
- High Mutativity + High Fertility = increased chance of rare trait mutations

### Inbreeding depression
Cloning the same plant too many generations in a row causes stat degradation.
Encourages players to crossbreed instead of just cloning the best plant.

## Gameplay

### Visual quality stages
Golden/enlarged crop models for plants with high stats.
Player can see the result of their breeding work visually in the world.

### Biome adaptation
Plants grown in the same climate for several generations gain Resistance bonus
to local temperature, but lose it when transplanted to a different biome.
Encourages region-specific farming and trade.

### Rare trait mutations
Not numeric stats, but unique properties:
- "Bioluminescent" - glows at night
- "Drought resistant" - doesn't require watering
- "Bountiful" - double harvest every 5th collection
- "Hardy" - survives one frost event without damage

Only appear through specific gene combinations or mutation chains.

### Seasonal bonuses
Certain gene combinations give bonuses in specific seasons:
- High Growth in spring = faster early growth
- High Resistance in winter = cold survival
- High Gain in autumn = harvest bonus
